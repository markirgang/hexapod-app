L298N & HC-SR04

DC Motor Driver & Ultrasonic Sensor Subsystems

L298N Dual H-Bridge DC Motor Driver

DC DRIVER
  • OUT 1 / OUT 2: Mouth Mechanism DC Motor Terminals
  • OUT 3 / OUT 4: Pulsating Neck Mechanism DC Motor Terminals
  • IN 1 / IN 2 / ENA: GPIO 23 / 24 / 18 (PWM Speed & Direction)
  • IN 3 / IN 4 / ENB: GPIO 25 / 8 / 19 (PWM Speed & Direction)
  • Power Terminals: +7.4V / +12V Battery Input (+) / Common GND / +5V Logic Output

4x HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Distance Sensors

SENSORS
  • Front Sensor: VCC = 5V | GND = GND | Trig = GPIO 5 | Echo = GPIO 6
  • Rear Sensor: VCC = 5V | GND = GND | Trig = GPIO 12 | Echo = GPIO 13
  • Left Sensor: VCC = 5V | GND = GND | Trig = GPIO 16 | Echo = GPIO 20
  • Right Sensor: VCC = 5V | GND = GND | Trig = GPIO 21 | Echo = GPIO 26
  • ⚠️ Voltage Protection Note: All HC-SR04 Echo pins use a 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor divider to step down 5V Echo signals to 3.3V Pi GPIO logic levels.

Hexapod Control Center Operating Manual & User Guide

Complete Setup, 3D Kinematics Pose Control, Servo Calibration, Gemini AI & Maintenance

Step 1

⚡ Quick Start & System Power On

  • 1. Power Supply Connection: Connect a 2S (7.4V) or 3S (11.1V) LiPo battery to the high-current power terminal bus. Ensure the 5V 10A UBEC buck regulator powers both PCA9685 boards and Raspberry Pi logic.
  • 2. Start Web Controller: Launch the Python server terminal command:
    python3 server.py
  • 3. Open Workbench: Navigate to http://localhost:8090 in your web browser.
  • 4. Connection Status: Ensure the status badge at top right displays ONLINE.
Step 2

🎮 3D Gait Kinematics & Pose Control

  • • Walking Gait Modes: Choose between Tripod (stable 3-leg gait), Run (fast gait), Ripple, and Wave gaits.
  • • Body Pose Sliders: Dynamically alter height offset (-40mm to +50mm), Pitch angle, Roll tilt, Yaw twist, and Stance Spread Width in real time.
  • • Step Geometry: Adjust Step Lift Height (15-60mm) and Stride Length (30-90mm) using the left panel sliders.
  • • Driving Shortcuts: Drive forward (W / ↑), reverse (S / ↓), turn left/right (A / D), and spin (Q / E). Press Spacebar to stop.
Step 3

🔧 18-Servo Trim Calibration Workbench

  • 1. Calibration Tab: Switch to 🔧 Servo Calibration view to inspect realtime 3D preview and fine-tune trim offsets.
  • 2. Joint Trim Adjustments: Calibrate individual offset angles (-20° to +20°) for Coxa (hip), Femur (thigh), and Tibia (knee) joints across all 6 legs.
  • 3. Quick Stance Presets: Test hardware alignment using quick buttons: Stand Neutral, Pancake Flat 🥞, Low Crouch, High Stance, Beg Position 🐶, and Bow / Respect.
Step 4

🤖 Gemini AI Multimodal Assistant & Camera Vision

  • 1. API Key Setup: Enter your Google Gemini API key in the AI panel or under ⚙️ Settings & HW.
  • 2. Activate Microphone: Click 🎙️ Activate Mic for live hands-free speech recognition with VU meter sensitivity gain control.
  • 3. Camera Scene Description: Click 👁️ Describe Scene to capture webcam frames and have Gemini speak environment descriptions out loud!
  • 4. Hand Gesture Control: Show 5 open fingers to camera to trigger greeting leg waves; show closed fist to halt motion.
Step 5

🎵 MP3 Music Player & Lip-Sync Dance Studio

  • 1. Import Music Files: Click 📁 Import MP3 to static/audio/ Folder to load songs into local storage.
  • 2. Associated Dance Routines: Select from routines including Alternating Front Leg Wave, Ultimate Combo Dance, Macarena, Catwalk Strut, K-Pop Groove, and Techno Shuffle.
  • 3. Playback & Lip-Sync: Click ▶️ Play & Lip-Sync. The mouth DC motor automatically opens during speech/song vocals!
Step 6

📡 Autonomous Avoidance & Safety Maintenance

  • • Ultrasonic Array: 4x HC-SR04 sensors continuously monitor 360° obstacle distances (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
  • • Auto Steering: Toggle Enable Ultrasonic Obstacle Avoidance in Settings. When enabled, the robot automatically steers around obstacles < 35 cm.
  • • Battery Protection: Disconnect LiPo battery when not in use. Verify 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor dividers step down 5V Echo signals to 3.3V Pi GPIO levels.
